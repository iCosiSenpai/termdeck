# Wallpaper generation briefs

All project-bound wallpaper assets were generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation workflow. These are the canonical art-direction briefs; shared constraints apply to every asset: 16:9 desktop composition, no text/logo/watermark/border, a dark low-detail field across the left 55%, and visual focus on the right for terminal readability.

## Core Collection

### Nordic Aurora 1.0.0

An original cinematic Arctic night: frozen fjord, angular mountains, a small modern observatory on the far-right ridge, and restrained aurora ribbons. Premium atmospheric matte painting with near-black polar blue, glacial cyan, muted aurora green, and tiny warm-gold accents.

### Cyber Circuit 1.0.0

An original cyberpunk circuit-city from a shadowed elevated walkway: luminous data rails, geometric megastructure, wet reflective surfaces, and subtle embossed circuitry. High-end industrial sci-fi with black glass, electric cyan, hot magenta, and sparing acid-lime.

### Tokyo Midnight 1.0.0

An original rain-soaked Tokyo-inspired rooftop overlooking a quiet futuristic city: elevated rail silhouettes, paper-lantern warmth, violet neon, and deep midnight mist. Cinematic anime-environment painting with no characters, signage, or fake lettering.

### Velvet Dusk 1.0.0

An original dreamlike twilight observatory garden: velvet-like plum clouds, crescent pavilion, rose glass, lavender foliage, and a few warm candles. Luxurious painterly fantasy with a refined editorial atmosphere.

### Ember Forge 1.0.0

An original monumental underground forge carved into black basalt: narrow molten-metal river, suspended steel rings, restrained smoke, and a single furnace on the far right. Cinematic dark-industrial concept art in charcoal, molten orange, amber, and tempered-steel blue.

### Carbon Mono 1.0.0

An original minimalist brutalist carbon-fiber interior: enormous graphite planes, a monolithic stair, subtle fog, and one thin mint light seam. Premium monochrome architectural visualization with museum-like silence.
